## Key Research Decisions (from research.md)

1. **No barrel re-exports** — NodeNext resolution won't auto-resolve `./types.js` to `./types/index.js`. Direct domain-specific imports are cleaner and avoid circular dependency risk.
2. **WorkflowContext record** — Bundles 7 immutable session params. Pure interface, not a class. Reduces parameter counts by 3-6 per extracted function.
3. **`currentTasks` is half-wired scaffolding** — Fix by re-parsing tasks after plan regeneration, not remove.
4. **Phase lives in core.ts, not tui.ts** — Eliminates `state.ts → tui.ts` dependency. Clean two-level DAG where state and TUI are independent siblings.
5. **13 dynamic imports to remove** — Planner `getVersion()` methods and cli.ts init have redundant dynamic imports of already-available modules.
6. **App has 19 useState (not 15)** — Extract into 3 hooks: useInteraction, useWorkflow, useAppNavigation. Residual App: ~80-100 lines.
