# Code of Conduct

## How this project is built

SPLITBRIEF is developed with SPLITBRIEF. The built CLI runs on its own repository: one AI coding tool plans and reviews, another builds, and a person watches every run. When a run fails or shows a defect, that failure becomes the next fix: the cause is found, fixed, verified with the project's gates, and checked again in a new run. [docs/DOGFOODING.md](./docs/DOGFOODING.md) describes the loop.

Contributions from people are welcome in this loop. A bug report from a real run, a failing case, or a fix with a test helps it improve. This code of conduct covers how people treat each other while they do that.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions, experience and points of view.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the users of the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would call inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@b4r7x](https://github.com/b4r7x), at <b4r7dev@gmail.com>. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code. The maintainer may also ban someone, for a time or permanently, for behaviour that is harmful.
